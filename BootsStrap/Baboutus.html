About Us

Society Survey and Representative Election Management System

The Society Survey and Representative Election Management System is designed to help residents participate in community decision-making through surveys and elections.

Our platform provides a transparent and secure way for society members to vote for representatives, participate in surveys, and stay informed about community activities.

The system enables residents to:

  • Register and manage their profiles
  • Participate in society surveys
  • Vote in representative elections
  • View candidate information
  • Check election results
  • Stay updated with announcements

Our goal is to encourage active participation, transparency, and effective communication within the community.
